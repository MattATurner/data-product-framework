# ADR-009: Provisional promotion policy

## Decision

A BRD may pass G0 with open questions only if each has an owner, a due date and a recorded assumption. Resulting products are provisional in the catalog until resolved.

## Consequences

Live assumptions force status provisional in data-product.v1 and the catalog registration.
